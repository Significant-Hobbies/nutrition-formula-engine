# Nutrition label reference brief

Accessed 2026-09-22.

## Search frame

The surface is an end-of-results label generator for formulation teams. It must preserve the
Clear Compliance Desk system, use familiar Indian retail-pack hierarchy, remain readable from
390 to 1440 pixels, add no runtime dependency, and keep theoretical or incomplete values visibly
qualified. Decorative packaging design, demographic dashboards, and claims unsupported by the
calculation are out of scope.

## References

### FSSAI Labelling and Display Regulations, Version VIII

- Source: https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Labelling%20Display_Version%20VIII_09_09_2025.pdf
- Surface: ingredient-list and nutritional-information requirements.
- Lesson: list ingredients in descending composition order; show nutrition per 100 g/100 mL or
  single-consumption pack and per-serving contribution to adult RDA; state serving size and
  servings per pack.
- Avoid: presenting a theoretical draft as a complete compliant pack declaration.

### Complan Pista Badam 500 g back panel

- Source: https://www.zydusmart.com/products/complan-nutrition-drink-powder-for-children-pista-badam-flavour-500-g-carton-nutrition-drink-for-kids-with-protein-34-vital-nutrients
- Surface: current retail nutrition-powder back panel.
- Lesson: one dense table can combine macronutrients, vitamins, minerals, per-100 values, and a
  declared reference without splitting the product into separate demographic labels.
- Avoid: copying brand colours, benefit claims, category claims, or proprietary pack artwork.

### Nestle India nutrition labelling guidance

- Source: https://www.nestle.in/csv/individuals-families/nutrition-information
- Surface: explanation of on-pack ingredient, portion, nutrition, and consumer-information layers.
- Lesson: make serving context and the ingredient statement easy to find, and keep consumer-care
  information separate from the nutrition table.
- Avoid: using corporate marketing language or front-of-pack badge styling in the calculation UI.

## Anti-reference

A men/women/child comparison matrix is useful in a technical report but is not the familiar retail
back-label pattern. It also hides the fact that child requirements change by age and, after age 10,
sex. The consumer label therefore uses one clearly footnoted adult reference.

## Direction principles

1. Use a conventional monochrome nutrition panel with strong rules and tabular numbers.
2. Keep per-100, per-serving, and adult `%RDA` columns visible together.
3. Preserve missing-data truth: partial values use a lower-bound marker and a written warning.
4. Keep editable product and serving details beside the preview, not inside a wizard.
5. Treat print/save-PDF as a draft handoff; keep regulatory completion outside the calculator.
